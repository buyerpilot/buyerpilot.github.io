Alibaba vs 1688: compare suppliers with marketplace-specific evidence

Alibaba and 1688 can both be useful for China sourcing, but they expose different seller and product information. BuyerPilot keeps the two marketplaces separate so their supplier rankings are based on the fields each platform actually provides.

Alibaba is designed for international B2B sourcing

Alibaba commonly presents export-oriented supplier information, Trade Assurance, seller and product ratings, business-type information and international buyer workflows. Available fields vary by seller and page.

1688 is a domestic China wholesale marketplace

1688 can expose different merchant, factory, repurchase and marketplace-status signals. Language and page structure also differ from Alibaba, which changes how supplier research needs to be interpreted.

Do not force both marketplaces into one formula

BuyerPilot uses marketplace-specific extraction and interpretation because the available evidence is not identical. A missing Alibaba field should not be treated as if the same field exists on 1688, and vice versa.

Compare the same business questions across both

Regardless of marketplace, buyers still need to evaluate product fit, quantity-aware pricing, supplier history, business type, marketplace status, samples, communication, compliance and commercial terms.

Use the platform that fits your sourcing process

Some buyers prefer Alibaba for international workflows, while others explore 1688 for domestic-market options or China-based sourcing support. BuyerPilot supports supplier shortlisting on both.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Is 1688 always cheaper than Alibaba?

Not necessarily. Listed prices, quantities, product specifications, tax treatment, domestic logistics, export handling and supplier terms can differ. Compare equivalent products and confirm final quotations.

Does BuyerPilot rank Alibaba and 1688 suppliers together?

No. BuyerPilot keeps Alibaba and 1688 processing separate because the marketplaces expose different fields and supplier signals.

Do I need a sourcing agent to use 1688?

That depends on your language, payment, export, logistics and supplier-management needs. BuyerPilot helps with browser-based supplier research but does not replace an agent, freight forwarder or importer of record.
