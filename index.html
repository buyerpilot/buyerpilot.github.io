The Founder's Story

Why Did I Build Sourcepilot?

Like many e-commerce sellers and independent brands, I spent countless hours staring at 1688 and Alibaba search grids, manually opening dozens of tabs just to compare suppliers. I was burnt out by hidden trading companies posing as direct manufacturers, messy product layouts, and spending weeks vetting suppliers only to find out their repurchase rates or return metrics didn't add up.

I realized that manual scrolling was costing hours of productivity and thousands of dollars in poor supplier matches.

So, I built Sourcepilot for myself to automate the pain away. It injects intelligence directly into the page you're already browsing—cutting out the noise and instantly surfacing the absolute best factories based on hard metrics. Now, I'm sharing it so you can skip the spreadsheet headache and source with total confidence.

How Does Sourcepilot Work?

Instead of forcing you to jump across software dashboards, Sourcepilot works right inside your active browsing session.

01

Non-Invasive Overlay

As you search or run image lookups on 1688 or Alibaba, Sourcepilot safely overlays neat checkboxes and metrics badges onto every active product grid card.

02

Automatic Data Gathering

It quietly captures key identifiers, pricing frameworks, and supplier indicators without altering or slowing down your page navigation experience.

03

Instant Ranking Panel

Open the side panel to view your weighted supplier ranking breakdown, letting you filter out middlemen and prioritize top-tier verified factories in a single click.

Mastering Your Settings

Understand the different configuration controls and follow this quick tutorial to get the most out of your workflow.

⚖️

Weight Profiles

Determines how the algorithm prioritizes suppliers when sorting your results list:

  • •
    Balanced: A healthy mix of pricing, historical ratings, and supplier tenure. Best for everyday product hunting.
  • •
    Factory-First: Heavily penalizes trading companies and ranks certified manufacturing plants at the absolute top.
⚙️

Ranking Modes

Controls how calculations are triggered on your target supplier pages:

  • •
    Automated Mode: Automatically scores and sorts cards as soon as your search results page finishes loading.
  • •
    Manual Mode: Lets you selectively check boxes on specific supplier cards to rank only the items you choose to compare.
