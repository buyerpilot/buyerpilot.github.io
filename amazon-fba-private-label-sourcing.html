Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Does BuyerPilot connect directly to Amazon Seller Central?

No. BuyerPilot is for supplier research on Alibaba and 1688. Amazon FBA and private-label sellers can use it upstream while sourcing products and manufacturers.

Can BuyerPilot calculate my final landed cost?

BuyerPilot compares available marketplace product pricing at the quantity entered. Final freight, duties, taxes, packaging, inspection and other landed-cost components should be calculated separately.

Is the cheapest Alibaba supplier usually the best private-label option?

Not necessarily. Price should be compared with product fit, MOQ, supplier history, business type, communication, sample quality, compliance, customization and other commercial factors.
