Tutorial 01

Rank Alibaba suppliers

Use Sourcepilot on a supported Alibaba results page without mixing Alibaba extraction or scoring with 1688 logic.

  1. Open a results page.Use an Alibaba keyword or image-search page that displays product cards.
  2. Select relevant products.Keep only listings that match your material, design, specification and intended order.
  3. Enter your quantity.Sourcepilot uses the applicable price tier for your intended quantity.
  4. Run Ranking.Seller pages are scanned for Alibaba-specific signals such as ratings, reorder rate, tenure, Trade Assurance, business type and revenue indicators.
Read tutorial transcript

On Alibaba, keep only relevant products selected. Enter the intended quantity so Sourcepilot can compare the applicable price tier. Run Ranking. Sourcepilot scans the selected seller pages for available Alibaba signals, processes them securely and returns one ranked entry for each unique supplier.

Tutorial 02

Rank 1688 suppliers

The 1688 workflow keeps its own seller-name translation, factory classification, merchant status, tenure and repeat-purchase logic.

  1. Open a supported 1688 results page.Sourcepilot appears alongside the product grid.
  2. Remove irrelevant offers.Deselect listings that should not influence the supplier comparison.
  3. Start the scan.Sourcepilot opens the required seller or product pages and pauses when verification must be completed manually.
  4. Review unique suppliers.Repeated products from the same seller are consolidated before ranking.
Read tutorial transcript

On 1688, remove offers that do not match your product requirement. Enter the quantity and run the scan. Sourcepilot processes Chinese supplier and product information, consolidates repeated offers from the same seller and displays an English-readable ranked shortlist.

Tutorial 03

Choose the ranking priority

The internal scoring profile remains marketplace-specific. The priority changes how strongly available factors influence the final comparison.

StandardCompares price, quality and supplier credibility together. Included in Free Trial and Starter.
Price FocusedPlaces more weight on competitive price at the selected quantity. Pro and Founder Lifetime.
Quality FocusedPlaces more weight on ratings, repeat purchase and credibility signals. Pro and Founder Lifetime.
Read tutorial transcript

Standard ranking balances price, supplier credibility and quality signals. Price Focused gives more importance to competitive pricing at the selected quantity. Quality Focused gives more importance to ratings, repeat-purchase and credibility signals. Advanced profiles require Pro or Founder Lifetime.

Tutorial 04

Export and verify ranking data

CSV export is visibly locked on Free Trial and Starter and is enforced by the Sourcepilot backend. Pro and Founder Lifetime unlock it.

  1. Complete a ranking.The export action appears after ranked suppliers are available.
  2. Select Export data (CSV).The extension checks the active plan before creating the file.
  3. Audit the calculation inputs.Review rank, score, score confidence, price, ratings, tenure, business type, verification and source URLs.
  4. Keep a permanent sourcing record.Open the file in Excel or Google Sheets to filter, compare and share supplier research.
Read tutorial transcript

Complete a supplier ranking with an eligible Pro or Founder Lifetime licence. Select CSV export. Sourcepilot verifies the plan and creates a file containing rank, score, pricing, supplier information, marketplace signals and source links for use in Excel or Google Sheets.

Tutorial 05

Start a trial or activate a paid licence

The extension popup provides trial signup, paid-key activation, plan status, usage, ranking settings and subscription management.

  1. Open the extension popup.Pin Sourcepilot to the browser toolbar for easier access.
  2. Start the Free Trial.Enter your email and WhatsApp/mobile number. The trial includes Standard ranking for seven days and up to 50 sessions.
  3. Activate a paid licence.Paste the Dodo licence key delivered after checkout.
  4. Manage access.Refresh, copy or deactivate the licence and open the billing portal from the same popup.
Read tutorial transcript

Open the Sourcepilot extension popup. Start the secure trial from the official trial page or paste a paid licence key. After activation, Sourcepilot stores device-bound tokens instead of the complete licence key. Use the popup to refresh access, open billing or deactivate the browser.
