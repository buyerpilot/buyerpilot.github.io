Works where sourcing research happens

The movable BuyerPilot panel appears on supported marketplace result pages, so users can select products and review ranked suppliers without maintaining a separate manual comparison first.

Protected server-side processing

Final normalisation, deduplication, ranking, plan enforcement and eligible CSV generation are handled by the BuyerPilot backend. The extension keeps only the browser-side logic required to read the supported pages and display results.

Three ranking priorities

Standard balances available pricing, credibility and quality signals. Pro and Founder Lifetime add Price Focused and Quality Focused priorities.

Available for three desktop browsers

BuyerPilot is prepared for Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Mozilla Firefox. Public store links are displayed on the homepage after each listing is approved.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about a supplier ranking extension built for marketplace research

Is BuyerPilot free?

The extension is free to install and offers a seven-day trial with up to 50 Standard ranking sessions. Optional paid plans add higher limits and advanced features.

Does BuyerPilot store the complete licence key?

After secure activation, the extension uses device-bound authentication tokens and does not retain the complete reusable licence key.

Is BuyerPilot affiliated with Alibaba or 1688?

No. BuyerPilot is an independent sourcing-assistance product.
