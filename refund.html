Refund Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026

This template uses a seven-day first-purchase refund window. Confirm that this is the policy you want and that it matches your Lemon Squeezy settings before publishing.

1. Free trial

Sourcepilot provides a seven-day Full Trial with all Pro features and a maximum of 50 ranking sessions. The trial is intended to let you test marketplace compatibility and the core ranking workflow before paying.

2. First paid purchase

You may request a refund for your first Sourcepilot subscription payment or Founder Lifetime purchase within seven calendar days of the payment date. Requests should include the purchase email, order number and a brief explanation of the issue.

3. Subscription renewals

Subscription renewals are generally non-refundable once processed. You can cancel future renewals through the payment provider before the next billing date. Duplicate charges, billing errors and mandatory legal rights remain eligible for review.

4. Technical problems

If Sourcepilot cannot function on supported marketplace pages because of a reproducible technical issue, contact support with the browser version, marketplace URL, screenshots and relevant error information. We may first attempt to resolve the problem before processing a refund.

5. Situations not normally eligible

  • Change of mind after the seven-day refund period.
  • Failure to cancel before a subscription renewal.
  • Marketplace CAPTCHA, verification or account restrictions outside Sourcepilot's control.
  • Data fields that Alibaba or 1688 do not display or disclose.
  • Disagreement with a supplier, order outcome or marketplace decision.
  • Licence suspension caused by abuse or violation of the Terms.

6. How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method by the payment provider. Processing time depends on the provider and your bank.

7. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits refund or cancellation rights that cannot legally be excluded in your jurisdiction.

8. Contact

Send refund requests to support.
