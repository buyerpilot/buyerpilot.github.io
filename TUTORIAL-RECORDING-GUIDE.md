# Sourcepilot real tutorial recording guide

The existing short animated videos are visual explainers. A genuine human-style tutorial should use a real browser recording from an authenticated Alibaba/1688 session. Record the raw clips below at 1920×1080, 100% browser zoom, with notifications closed and no personal information visible.

## Video 1 — Complete Sourcepilot workflow (3–4 minutes)
1. Open the extension popup and show an active licence.
2. Open a supported 1688 or Alibaba results page.
3. Select and deselect product cards.
4. Enter an intended quantity.
5. Click Run Ranking.
6. Show seller tabs opening and the progress state.
7. Show the final deduplicated supplier shortlist.
8. Open one product/source link.

## Video 2 — Alibaba supplier ranking (2–3 minutes)
Record the entire flow on a real Alibaba search-results page, including product selection, quantity, scan and results.

## Video 3 — 1688 supplier ranking (2–3 minutes)
Record the entire flow on a real 1688 results page, including English-readable seller results and duplicate consolidation.

## Video 4 — Ranking profiles (60–90 seconds)
Open the popup, explain Standard, Price Focused and Quality Focused, save each setting and show the locked state on a Trial/Starter plan.

## Video 5 — CSV export (60–90 seconds)
Using a Pro/Lifetime licence, complete a ranking, export CSV, open it in Excel or Google Sheets and point out rank, score, confidence, price and source links.

## Video 6 — Trial and licence activation (60–90 seconds)
Open the trial page, complete Turnstile, show the emailed key, activate it once in the extension, then show plan status and deactivation. Use a test email and blur the full key.

## Recording style
- Speak naturally and explain why each click matters.
- Keep the mouse movement slow and deliberate.
- Pause for one second after each important result.
- Do not show passwords, API keys, personal emails, phone numbers or licence keys.
- Use one continuous recording per tutorial; avoid a slideshow of screenshots.
