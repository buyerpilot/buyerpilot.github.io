# BuyerPilot human tutorial scripts

These scripts are written for real screen recordings, not slideshow videos. Speak naturally; do not read every sentence word-for-word if it sounds unnatural.

## Video 1 — Complete BuyerPilot workflow (3–4 minutes)

**Opening**

“Hi, this is BuyerPilot. In this video I’ll show you how to turn a crowded Alibaba or 1688 search page into a ranked shortlist of unique suppliers.”

**Extension setup**

“First, pin BuyerPilot to your browser toolbar. Open the popup and confirm that your trial or paid licence is active. Standard ranking is included in the trial. Price Focused, Quality Focused and CSV export are available on eligible plans.”

**Product selection**

“Now open a supported marketplace results page. BuyerPilot adds a checkbox beside each product card. Keep only the products that genuinely match your requirement. Deselect different materials, incorrect designs and irrelevant variations, because those products should not influence your supplier comparison.”

**Quantity**

“Enter the quantity you expect to order. This matters because products can have several price tiers. BuyerPilot compares the pricing that applies to the quantity you enter instead of blindly using the lowest advertised price.”

**Run ranking**

“Click Run Ranking. BuyerPilot opens the required seller pages and gathers the marketplace information available for the selected products. If Alibaba or 1688 shows a verification page, BuyerPilot pauses so you can complete it manually.”

**Results**

“When the scan is complete, BuyerPilot consolidates repeated products from the same seller. That means one supplier cannot occupy several ranks simply because it published many similar listings. You can now review the unique suppliers, final scores, confidence, relevant product links and available supplier signals.”

**Closing**

“Use the shortlist to focus your supplier conversations. Confirm your specifications, request samples and complete normal commercial verification before placing an order.”

## Video 2 — Alibaba supplier ranking (2–3 minutes)

“Open an Alibaba keyword or image-search results page and wait for the BuyerPilot panel to appear. Select only the product listings that match the item you want to source. Enter your intended quantity, then click Run Ranking.

BuyerPilot opens the selected Alibaba seller or product pages and gathers available information such as product pricing, ratings, years on Alibaba, reorder signals, Trade Assurance, business type, certification and revenue indicators. The protected backend normalises the records and consolidates repeated sellers before calculating the final shortlist.

When the ranking is ready, review the highest-ranked suppliers first. Open the relevant product and supplier links, contact the shortlisted company and confirm the final quotation, product variation, minimum order, lead time and sample terms.”

## Video 3 — 1688 supplier ranking (2–3 minutes)

“Open a supported 1688 search-results page. Use BuyerPilot’s checkboxes to remove products that do not match your required material, design or specification. Enter your intended quantity and start the ranking.

BuyerPilot processes the available 1688 seller information, including company-name candidates, years on platform, merchant status, repurchase information, factory or trading indicators, product prices and other available marketplace signals. Repeated offers from the same seller are consolidated into one supplier entry.

The result is an English-readable supplier shortlist with direct source links. Use it to reduce the number of sellers you need to inspect manually, then confirm the commercial details with the shortlisted supplier.”

## Video 4 — Ranking profiles (60–90 seconds)

“Open the BuyerPilot popup and go to Ranking settings.

Standard ranking balances available price, quality and supplier-credibility signals. It is included in the Free Trial and Starter plan.

Price Focused gives more importance to competitive pricing at the quantity you selected. Quality Focused gives more importance to ratings, repeat-purchase and credibility signals. These advanced profiles are available on Pro and Founder Lifetime.

Choose the profile that matches your current sourcing objective and click Save ranking setting before starting the next scan.”

## Video 5 — CSV export (60–90 seconds)

“Complete a supplier ranking using a Pro or Founder Lifetime licence. When the results are ready, select Export CSV.

BuyerPilot verifies your plan and creates the file on the protected backend. Open the CSV in Excel or Google Sheets. You can review rank, score, confidence, applicable price, ratings, tenure, business type, marketplace status, repeat-purchase information and source links where available.

Use filters and notes to create a permanent sourcing record or share the shortlist with your team.”

## Video 6 — Trial and licence activation (60–90 seconds)

“Open the BuyerPilot extension popup. To begin the free trial, select Start secure free trial. The official BuyerPilot trial page opens in a new tab. Enter your email and mobile number, complete Cloudflare verification and submit the form.

BuyerPilot emails the trial licence key to you. Copy the key, return to the extension popup, paste it once and select Activate licence. After secure activation, the complete key is not stored inside the extension. BuyerPilot uses device-bound authentication tokens instead.

Paid customers follow the same activation step using the licence key delivered after checkout. Use the popup to refresh access, open billing or deactivate the browser.”
